# gfx-wah

> **Status: CODED** (v0.1.0, 2026-11-12) — eleven voices from golem's measured tables (+ Classic, Clyde, Bass 105Q), each pedal's own pot law (Vox heel = low), EXP-smoothed pedal, taper, heel / toe, Q and range scaling, antialiased drive, auto-engage, manual / auto / alien; 5 behaviour tests, bench green (step bound −35 dB: a fast sweep of a +20 dB resonance is a chirp, not a click).

golem's wah. Stands for two golem blocks whose ids never conflict ([golem contract](../docs/golem-contract.md)):

| golem block | Today | Catalogue ids |
|---|---|---|
| `wah` | guitarix GCB-95 Cry Baby | `wah` 0 – 1 (pedal, heel → toe), `volume` −20 … +4 dB |
| `wah-color` | guitarix colwah (7 modelled wahs) | `model` (Colorsound, Dallas, Foxx, Jen, Maestro, Selmer, Vox V847), `wah` 0 – 1, `freq` 24 – 360, `mix` 0 – 100 %, `mode` (manual, auto, alien) |

The auto-wah is [gfx-autowah](../gfx-autowah/): there `wah` means sensitivity, so it cannot share this
plugin. The aim: every classic wah voice, a pedal that feels like a pedal under golem's EXP (no
staircase, no lag), auto-engage, and the resonance's own clipping.

## 1. What golem has today

From guitarix's Faust sources and golem's JSFX [V]:

- Each colwah model is a linear 4th-order IIR whose coefficients are polynomials in the pedal and the
  sample rate, generated by guitarix's DK-method builder from schematics (GPL-2+). GCB-95: linear model,
  peak 408 Hz (heel) → 2,250 Hz (toe; golem-rack measured Gxgcb_95, 2026-11-12), Q 8.5 → 3.1, about +19 dB at the peak.
- **`freq` is not in Hz**: guitarix defines it as the "alien" LFO in **BPM** (24 – 360 = 0.4 – 6 Hz). The
  catalogue's unit is wrong (golem side: fix to BPM); `golem_colwah.jsfx` already treats it as BPM.
- Pedal laws differ per model (Colorsound inverted log 5, Foxx log 1, Maestro inverted log 1, Selmer
  linear, Vox linear and **not inverted**). `golem_colwah.jsfx` applies one law to all, so Foxx, Maestro,
  Selmer and Vox have the wrong taper; **Vox runs backwards** (toe at wah = 0) in guitarix and in the JSFX.
- guitarix smooths the pedal over ~3 ms: with EXP updates every 10 – 20 ms, a high-Q resonance buzzes at the
  control rate.

## 2. State of the art

Survey of 2026-11-12. **[V]** read in the source; **[I]** inference; **[U]** not checked.

| Reference | Facts | What we take |
|---|---|---|
| Holters & Zölzer, *Physical Modelling of a Wah-wah Effect Pedal…*, DAFx 2011 ([PDF](https://www.hsu-hh.de/ant/wp-content/uploads/sites/699/2017/10/Holters-Zölzer-2011-Physical-Modelling-of-a-Wah-wah-Effect-Pedal-as-a-Case-Study-for-Application-of-the-Nodal-DK-Method-to-Circuit.pdf)) [V] | Cry Baby by the nodal DK method (500 mH inductor); very sensitive to tolerances; **the peak gain clips even at moderate levels** | Clipping at the resonance |

| Fractal wah ([wiki](https://wiki.fractalaudio.com/gen1/index.php?title=Wah)) [V] | Track (Q falls as frequency rises); Clyde = 369 – 2,369 Hz, Q 4.17; auto-engage with an off position and hysteresis | Clyde voice, auto-engage |

### What we conclude

1. **Model each pedal by its response, not its 4th-order coefficients**: tabulate guitarix's responses
   (fc, Q, peak gain, floor, slope, high-pass corner) over the pedal travel and play them back with a TPT
   state-variable filter (low-pass + band-pass mix) plus a one-pole high-pass. A time-varying direct-form
   4th-order filter is ill-conditioned in f32.
2. **Each pedal's own taper**, Vox fixed to heel = low.
3. **EXP smoothing as a ramp over the measured update interval**, not a fixed one-pole.
4. **Clipping at the resonance** (ADAA soft clip).
5. Auto-engage by pedal position, with hysteresis and a wait.
6. Skipped: full nonlinear DK in real time, inductor saturation beyond the soft clip, pickup / fuzz
   input-impedance interaction (golem plays from a DI).

## 3. Design

- **Voices**: the catalogue's seven (Colorsound, Dallas, Foxx, Jen, Maestro, Selmer, Vox V847) in their
  order, then appended: **GCB-95** (the default — the `wah` block sends no `model`), Classic (Smith's fit),
  Clyde (Fractal's settings), Bass 105Q [U voicing].
- **Pedal path**: EXP value → ramp over the measured update interval (median of the last few) → 2nd-order
  critically damped smoother (3 – 5 ms) → slew limit (full travel ≥ 15 ms) → heel / toe limits → taper →
  per-sample filter update.
- **Modes**: manual (the pedal), auto (an envelope follower drives the position — gfx-autowah's follower),
  alien (an LFO at `freq` BPM, catalogue meaning).
- **Auto-engage**: below `engage_pos` (with hysteresis) for `engage_wait`, the wah fades out over 15 ms
  internally; any movement brings it back.

## 5. Applications

| Use | Settings to start from | Feature that matters |
|---|---|---|
| Classic wah solo (Hendrix, Clapton) | GCB-95 or Vox V847 or Clyde, EXP on `wah`, `drive` 30 % | Per-model taper; EXP smoothing without staircase |
| Funk rhythm "wacka" | Classic, `q` 150 % | |
| Wah into high gain (Hammett) | GCB-95, `q` 80 %, `volume` +3 dB, before the amp | Boost; clipping at the resonance |
| Parked wah (Schenker, Knopfler) | `wah` fixed at 0.4 – 0.6, `mode` manual | A fixed resonant mid voice |
| Step on it, it engages | `auto_engage` on, `engage_pos` 3 %, `engage_wait` 300 ms | Switchless (Morley) |
| Smaller foot travel | `heel` 10 %, `toe` 85 % | |
| Bass wah | Bass 105Q, `mix` 70 % | Lows kept by the mix |
| Auto-wah without a pedal | `mode` auto, `env_sens` 60 % | Envelope drives the same models |
| Rhythmic "alien" sweep | `mode` alien, `freq` 120 BPM | |
| Vintage colours | Colorsound, Maestro, Foxx, Jen, Selmer, Dallas | 7 circuit-derived voicings |
