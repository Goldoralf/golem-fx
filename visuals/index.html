golem-fx

Golem's own effects, in two finishes: Thrash (gloss lacquer, diamond plate round the switch, the motif in yellow lined in black) for the aggressive families (drive, dynamics, utility, plus the killswitch) and Anodized (machined aluminium in the family's colour, the motif engraved in the metal) for the rest (the gap meter included). The finish switch can force one for every pedal. Each effect carries a motif that says what it does, and its glyph lights as a rune when the pedal is on. Knobs, selectors and their default positions come from each effect's code (gfx-bench manifest).

Other golem blocks

Every other effect golem hosts gets the same vintage enamel box. The glyph on its badge tells them apart; its colour comes from the block's family, aged.

Glyphs

One glyph per catalogue type, drawn on a 24 px grid in the stroke style of golem's icons. Families share a motif: captures sit in a hexagon, reverbs open outwards, delays repeat to the right. The faded character is the former Unicode glyph, kept as a text fallback.
