golem-fx

Golem's own effects, in two finishes: Thrash (gloss lacquer, diamond plate round the switch, the motif in yellow lined in black) for the aggressive families (drive, dynamics, utility, plus the killswitch) and Anodized (machined aluminium in the family's colour, the motif engraved in the metal) for the rest (the gap meter included). The high-gain companions (Met, Emet, Shem) and the vocal doubler (Golem) wear Blackwork: black lacquer, white engraving, a skull whose eyes light when the pedal is on. The finish switch can force one for every pedal. Each effect carries a motif that says what it does. Its glyph is engraved in the metal like the motif, always at the same height so a row of pedals lines up, and lights as a rune when the pedal is on; the selectors sit right under the knobs. Knobs, selectors and their default positions come from each effect's code (gfx-bench manifest).

Blackwork: the high-gain companions and Golem

Four pedals in their own black finish. Three are built for the front of a high-gain amp, designed from what three Fortin / Neural DSP pedals do (as new sounds, not clones), in their own Blackwork finish: black lacquer, white engraving, a skull whose eyes light when the pedal is on. Met is a keyed expander-gate that listens to the clean DI (the Zuul's role); Emet a high-headroom 808 overdrive (the Hexdrive's); Shem a clean, shaped tight boost (the Grind's). Golem, for the voice lane, doubles a singer one octave below with the formants kept or moved (the Sleep Token Emergence sound): eyes violet, voice arcs round the skull, presets Emergence, Emergence dark, Whammy voice, Choir, Unison double, Harmony third. Try them on the High gain and Voice lanes above.

Outboard (19" rack)

NAM captures of preamps, compressors and EQs as rack units (namRack), one per pack. The controls show the setting the capture was taken at, read from its name (gain, hard, channel, ratio); unknown gear gets a generic unit voiced by kind.

Outboard in the Designer (pedal footprint)

The same units as 500-series-style modules (namModule), at a pedal's size, for the Designer's pedal mode: the capture's own setting first, then the meter and the other controls while the column has room.

Outboard IR (ir-fx)

golem's IR Effect plays a captured unit's impulse response whole: spring tanks, tape and analog echoes, studio boxes. format: 'ir' draws the same units with an engraved IR mark, the knobs an IR cannot turn greyed, and the program the capture was taken on (program, or read from the label: "Spring Reverb 3", "delay 7") on the unit's display: a stepped selector, an LED readout or a lit LCD. Units without a display get an LED readout; unknown gear gets a generic digital reverb or echo. The last rack unit is off: its display goes dark. Click a module to switch it off.

Keys

golem's keys sounds (keysUnit), one drawing per family, for golem.fm's /library/keys: a black-lacquer upright for the acoustic piano, a graphite 80s FM keyboard for the E-Piano, a walnut tonewheel organ with its drawbars, and a synth whose panel takes the preset's style colour, its screen the style's wave and its knobs the preset's four macros. Electric pianos take a family from family or from the program's title (keysFamily): FM, tine, reed, clav, harpsichord, mallet. The name on the panel comes from title; a period and a material, never a maker's livery. On, the keys hold a chord.

Compact (a wall of sounds)

keysUnit({compact: true}): a 300-wide tile, readable at ~230 px. The title large on the family's material, two octaves of keys (bars for a mallet sound), the synth's style wave and its four macros as bars.

Kits

golem's drum kits, for Library ▸ Kits: drum sets (drumKit): one photograph per kind of kit (rock, jazz, metal, vintage, electronic, hybrid), seen from the drummer's throne, generated for golem with no livery or lettering, the title in the strip; and pad kits (padKit), a 4 × 4 controller with one label and colour per pad. lit lights what plays (kick, snare, hats, ride, crash, toms; or pad indexes). Never a maker's livery.

golem features

golem's service blocks are golem's own devices, not effects, and get a family of their own (featureUnit, the Console look): Rec, Tuner, Click, Backing Track, Looper, Riff, Drum Machine, Standard, Arrangement and Sample. A graphite unit the size of a pedal, a light bar in the block's colour, a screen that shows what the feature is doing, and a ribbed rubber pad in a lit well. No knobs, no chrome stomp. pedalFor draws them so. Click a unit to switch it off.

Hardware

The MIDI hardware golem drives, from its pedal registry (hardwareUnit): one realistic render per kind of unit, at the real units' proportions, with no maker's livery, logo or lettering. golem's Whammy DT block draws as the pitch treadle; a MIDI pedal takes the kind its registry entry names. Click a unit to switch it off.

Other golem blocks

Every other effect golem hosts gets the same vintage enamel box. The glyph on its badge tells them apart (golem's own features excepted: see above); its colour comes from the block's family, aged.

Glyphs

One glyph per catalogue type, drawn on a 24 px grid in the stroke style of golem's icons. Families share a motif: captures sit in a hexagon, reverbs open outwards, delays repeat to the right. The faded character is the former Unicode glyph, kept as a text fallback.
