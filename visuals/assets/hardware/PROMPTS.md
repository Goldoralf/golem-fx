# Hardware pedal photographs: generation prompts

> **Status: PROMPTS READY** (2026-11-15), images not generated yet. One generic photograph per *kind* of MIDI-
> controlled hardware golem can drive (golem's pedal registry, `library/pedals/*.json`). Generic on purpose: a
> maker's product photo is someone else's copyright and its logo a trademark, so neither ships in golem's public
> repository. Each image shows the *form* of a kind of unit, never a maker's livery, colours or lettering.

Files, once generated by the maintainer with Nano Banana (web): `<kind>.webp` (1600 px wide) and `<kind>-sm.webp`
(600 px), like the drum kits in `assets/kits/`. Every prompt shares the same camera and light paragraph so the set
stays consistent; keep it word for word when regenerating one.

## Shared paragraph (in every prompt)

> Photorealistic studio product photograph, straight top-down view from directly above, the unit centred and
> fully visible with a margin around it, lying flat on a plain matte charcoal-grey seamless background, soft even
> diffused studio light from above with a gentle soft contact shadow, crisp focus over the whole unit, realistic
> materials and small wear. Absolutely no logos, no brand names, no model names, no text, no letters, no numbers
> and no printed labels anywhere on the unit: blank control labels, blank screens show only simple abstract bars
> or a plain glow.
